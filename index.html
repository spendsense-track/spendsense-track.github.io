Privacy Policy Overview

SpendSense is built with a privacy-first architecture. All your shopping lists, item details, monthly budgets, and spending metrics are stored locally on your device. We do not operate external databases, run analytics servers, or sell your personal data.

  • 100% Local Storage: All scanned grocery items, category structures, receipt logs, and monthly profiles stay inside your device storage.
  • Notifications & Reminders: System notifications and bill reminders are scheduled entirely locally on your device via Android AlarmManager/WorkManager. No remote push servers are used.
  • Contacts & Group Bill Privacy: Device contacts permission (READ_CONTACTS) is 100% optional, read on-device only for picking group members, and never uploaded to remote servers.
  • On-Device Receipt Scanning: Temporary scanned images are processed locally and deleted immediately after item extraction.
  • Data Ownership & Reset: Export JSON backups anytime or reset all app data with a single tap.
