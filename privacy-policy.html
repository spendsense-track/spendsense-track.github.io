SpendSense Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 8, 2026 (v1.1.6). Your privacy is under your absolute control.

SpendSense is built with a privacy-first architecture. All your shopping lists, item details, monthly budgets, and spending metrics are stored locally on your device. We do not operate external databases, run analytics servers, or sell your personal data.

Flexible Storage (Offline & Cloud Sync)

Guest users enjoy 100% offline local device storage. Registered users signing in via Google Authentication can optionally sync their receipts and budgets securely to Google Firebase Firestore cloud storage for multi-device access.

Gemini Chat AI Safety

Conversations with the AI budget assistant are kept offline on your device. Chat payloads are sent securely to Google Gemini APIs only when submitting queries and are never logged or stored by us.

On-Device Receipt Scanning

Invoice text reading is processed on-device. The text matches are temporarily formatted via Google Gemini APIs to populate your lists. Captured receipt images are deleted immediately after scanning.

Data Backup & Ownership

You maintain complete ownership of your historical metrics. You can export a JSON backup file to your own storage at any time and import it to restore your profile on a new device.

Coin Economy & Anti-Fraud Policy

SpendSense uses a transparent Coin Economy for premium AI services. Manual entry is 100% free and ad-free. Coins are deducted ONLY upon successful feature execution (5 🪙 per AI Scan/Voice/Chat/CSV export, 100 🪙 per Cloud Backup). Rewarded ads are strictly limited to 10 per day with a 15-second cooldown.

Group Splitter & Local Contacts

Track shared bills (trips, dining, flatmates, office lunches) with Equal, Custom, Percentage, and Shares calculations. Phonebook contacts are accessed 100% locally on-device. No contact data is ever uploaded, shared, or sold.

Firebase Auth & Cloud Sync

Guest data remains 100% local on device. Registered users can optionally authenticate via Google / Firebase Sign-In for cloud backup sync. Account data is transmitted via encrypted HTTPS directly to Firebase Firestore.

Coin Economy & Monetization Rules

To provide a fair, transparent, and Google Play & AdMob compliant experience, SpendSense operates under strict monetization policies:

  • 100% Ad-Free Manual Entry: Creating, editing, and saving expense items manually is 100% free and never triggers interstitial ads, rewarded ads, or coin charges.
  • Success-Only Coin Deductions: Coins are deducted ONLY when an operation completes successfully and results are delivered to you. Zero coins are charged if an operation fails or is cancelled by the user.
  • Feature Coin Pricing: AI Voice Parsing (5 🪙), Camera/Gallery OCR Scan (5 🪙), CSV Export (5 🪙), PDF Statement Export (5 🪙), AI Financial Insights (5 🪙), AI Chat Assistant (5 🪙), Scheduled Bill Reminders (5 🪙), and Full Cloud Firestore Database Backup (100 🪙).
  • AdMob Anti-Fraud Limits: Rewarded ad watches are limited to a maximum of 10 ads per day per user, with a mandatory 15-second cooldown timer between requests to prevent fraudulent click behavior.
  • Sign-In Bonus: Users receive a one-time +100 Coins Bonus upon registering or signing in via Firebase / Google Authentication.

Information Collection & Use

We respect your privacy and request device permissions solely for essential local capabilities and optional cloud backup features:

  • Camera: Used to capture receipts for on-device OCR text extraction.
  • Location: Used locally to detect country and city for currency formatting and local grocery tips. This is foreground-only and never shared.
  • Microphone & Audio: Used only when you record voice notes for AI item entry. Audio is processed temporarily and not stored by us.
  • Device Contacts (`android.permission.READ_CONTACTS`): Optional permission used exclusively when you choose to import member names into the Group Splitter bill feature. Selected contact names are read 100% locally on your device. We NEVER upload, sync, sell, or transmit your contact book to any server. You can also manually type any member name without granting contacts access.
  • SMS Permissions (RECEIVE_SMS / READ_SMS): Used for automated local bank transaction SMS detection (e.g., ATM withdrawals & debit alerts). All SMS processing occurs 100% locally on your device; no SMS content is ever uploaded.
  • Firebase Authentication & Cloud Storage: Optional Google Sign-In collects your basic user profile (Email, Display Name, UID) to authenticate and securely back up your grocery receipts and budgets to Firebase Firestore.
  • Notifications: Used to display native local bill payment reminders and automated expense notifications on your device.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Used by Google AdMob SDK for ad serving and frequency cap enforcement.

Third-Party Services

SpendSense integrates industry-standard third-party SDKs:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): Used for secure user authentication and cloud database backup synchronization.
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): Used for banner ads and rewarded video ad delivery under Google's publisher policies.
  • Google Gemini API (Google LLC): Used to format invoice text and power AI assistant responses.

How You Can Control Your Data

Guest users can delete all local data at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage. Logged-in users can initiate full cloud backups, export CSV/JSON backups, or request account deletion from Settings.

Children's Privacy

SpendSense does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of any policy updates.
